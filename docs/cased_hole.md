# Cased-hole logs: production logging and integrity (cement bond) logs (Volve)

Code: `src/processing/cased_hole.py`, reader `logs.petro_dlis` / `petro_las` with the adapter section `cased_hole` (`config/adapters/volve.yaml`), limits in `config/borehole.yaml` (`cement`, `plt`); switches `logs_plt` and `logs_cement` (`scripts/volve_check.py --family logs_plt --family logs_cement`, heavy: 3.7 GB of DLIS, read in the work folder); marts `well_cement_profile`, `well_cement_summary`, `well_plt_profile`, `well_plt_gradient`, `well_plt_vs_gauge`; notebooks 22 section 6d (production logs) and 27 section 8b (cement).

## 1. Production logs (`10.PRODUCTION LOGS`, 435 files, 10 wellbore folders, 2009-2016)
Runs of three kinds: flow surveys (caliper, collar locator, spinner, holdup, pressure, temperature, fluid density; F-1 C 2014, F-11 B 2015, F-12 2009 and 2010, F-14 2009 and 2010), injector pressure-temperature logs (F-4, F-5), and perforating or plug-setting runs (CCL-PERF, CCL-PLUG). Only pressure (`WPRE`, `BHPR`, `QGCP`; units declared), temperature, fluid density and the spinner are read; every other channel is catalogued by the log catalogue. A channel whose unit is not declared is not converted (`QGPF` is a frequency in Hz and is not read).
- **Pressure gradient** (`well_plt_gradient`): the pressure of a run is pooled on a 5 m grid over its passes (median and spread between passes), tied to TVD below sea level with the selected survey, and a straight line (Theil-Sen) is fitted in windows of 200 m TVD (step 100 m). A window is fitted only with 8 bins, 50 m of vertical span and a mean spread between passes below 3 bar. The equivalent density classes the slope (assumed limits: gas-like below 0.04 bar/m, oil or gas-oil mixture, water-like 0.085-0.115, above that not a static fluid column: pressure changed in time, as in a memory-gauge run during perforating). 176 of 200 windows were fitted in 28 runs.
- **What it shows:** oil-like columns 0.070-0.085 bar/m at 2,000-2,800 m TVD (F-12 2009, F-14 2009, F-11 B 2015), a water-like column 0.096-0.11 (F-1 C 2014-11-28, a shut-in well), a gas-like upper part in F-11 B 2015. This is the evidence for the gradient band of the gauge shut-in correction (`production.shutin.fluid_gradient_band_bar_per_m`, now 0.07-0.1005 bar/m, was 0.06-0.1005 as an assumption). The fluid in the well on the date of a given shut-in is not known.
- **Log against the permanent gauge** (`well_plt_vs_gauge`): only a STATIONARY log pressure (a bin read by at least two passes, spread below 3 bar, within 150 m of the gauge MD) is compared, carried to the gauge TVD with the measured gradient of the nearest window. Only 1 of 11 log days qualifies (15/9-F-15 D, 2016-07-13: log 297 bar, gauge daily average 346 bar, shut in, a perforating run that week). The others reach the gauge depth on a single moving pass or not at all. In 15/9-F-1 C on 2014-11-28 a stationary pressure at 2,772 m (271 bar, 7 passes, spread 12.8 bar, so not counted) carried 75 m up with the 0.096 bar/m of its window gives 264 bar against the gauge's 264.6 bar.
- **Interventions:** perforating and plug runs of the folders are matched to the gauge shut-in episodes (a day either side; `volve_shutin_pressure.intervention_logged`): 6 of 106 episodes. During an intervention the well may be killed with a heavy fluid and the gauge reads that column. The 73-day, flat shut-in of 15/9-F-15 D from 2016-07-07 (346 bar, above the initial pressure) has a perforating run on 2016-07-13 and is NOT read as a reservoir pressure; the log gradient on that day is 0.1006 bar/m, equal to the seawater gradient.

## 2. Integrity (cement bond) logs (`11. INTEGRITY LOGS`, 9 files, 4 wells)
Wireline runs with the provider's CBL (`BI`, `CBL`, `VDL`) and ultrasonic (`USBI`, `USGI`, `AIAV`, `ECCE`, `ERAV`, waveforms) channels: 15/9-F-9 (2009-06-26), 15/9-F-11 B (2013-06-05), 15/9-F-15 C (2013-10-09), 15/9-F-12 (2016-08-18). `well_cement_summary` gives per run the logged interval, the shares of the bins in each class of the bond index (good at or above 0.8, moderate from 0.5: ASSUMED limits, requests req.101 and req.102), the longest stretch of good bond (gaps up to 2 m), the median impedance and eccentering. Results: good share 25-97 % by run, longest good stretch 83-236 m in the 2013 and 2016 runs; 15/9-F-9 (a shallow section) is lowest at about 25 %; the CBL index of the F-15 C run of 2013 is 1.0 in 100 % of 2,902 bins from 146 m: a default, flagged `index_looks_constant`, not used.
- **What a bond log cannot say** (explicit in every table): it shows the bond at the casing-cement interface; it does not prove isolation (channelling, microannulus, cement type), and no barrier acceptance rule (NORSOK D-010) has been read, so a bond class is not a barrier verdict. The cement jobs (type, volume, date, top of cement) are not in the data read. The Sodir-only statements in `docs/well_construction.md` ("no bond log") hold for the open Sodir data; the Volve archive is the exception.

## 3. Limits and data to request
Which tool was in the hole and whether a pass was stationary (a station table or the log header), the time of each pass, the gauge calibration, the cement job reports and the kill fluid of the 2016 interventions, the provider's bond-log interpretation reports (the archive holds PDF reports not read here), and the reviewed standards (req.101, req.102).
